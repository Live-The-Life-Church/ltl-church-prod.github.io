About Us

The next generation matters!

We understand that children learn best when given age-specific environments and programs, making LTL Kids a place where kids want to come every week. Kids are challenged every week in service to trust God, make wise choices, and build lasting friendships.

The safety of your family is important to us. All LTL Kids services feature a secure electronic check-in system, trained teachers, and dedicated security personnel to ensure your children are safe and secure while taking part in LTL Kids. All workers have gone through a process of training and background checks to be qualified to serve your kids.

Age Groups

Built for every age!

From the tiniest nursery snuggles to full-throttle elementary worship — every age gets a space made just for them.

0 – 24 mo

Nursery

A calm, secure room for our littlest guests — soft rockers, gentle music, and lots of snuggles.

2 – 5 yrs

Preschool

Songs, stories, crafts, and play that introduce little hearts to a big God.

K – 5th

Elementary

High-energy worship, wild games, and small-group time built for their world.

Check In

LTL Kids check-in opens 30 minutes before each service, so we encourage parents to arrive early for an easy check-in process.

For easiest access to LTL Kids check-in, we encourage all parents to park in the back, where you'll be greeted by our LTL Kids team!

03

Safety

Every service features a secure electronic check-in system, trained teachers, and dedicated security personnel to keep your kids safe and secure.
