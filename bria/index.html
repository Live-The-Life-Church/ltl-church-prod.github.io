The class

Wise choices about relationships.

Bri.A is a 13 lesson class, where youth (ages 14-17) and young adults (ages 18-24) discover how to make wise choices about their relationships.

We support the next generation by providing evidence-based programming that covers knowing yourself, forming healthy relationships, recognizing unhealthy and dangerous ones, navigating technology, and effective communication.

Bri.A also includes a component that promotes parent/trusted adult connections to help youth build trusted relationships with the adults in their life, outside and beyond our program.

What we cover

Evidence-based programming.

01

Knowing Yourself

02

Forming & Maintaining Healthy Relationships

03

Recognizing Unhealthy & Dangerous Relationships

04

05

Effective Communication & Conflict Management Skills
