Questions About Giving?

Good to know.

Email giving@ltl.church
Why do we give?
Giving is an act of worship and obedience to God. It's how we honor Him with what He's entrusted to us and partner together to see lives changed. Your generosity helps us reach people, serve our community, and advance the mission of the church.
How can I give?
You can give online through our website, through the Church Center app (after selecting LTL Church as your home church), in person during service, or by mail. Our easiest and most secure option is giving online or through the app.
Is online giving secure?
Yes. Our giving platform uses industry-standard encryption and security to protect your information.
Can I set up recurring giving automatically?
Absolutely. Recurring giving is a simple way to stay consistent in your generosity. You can set it up through our website or the Church Center app and adjust it anytime.
What is tithing?
A tithe is traditionally the first 10% of income given back to God. It's a way we put Him first and trust Him with our finances.
Where does my giving go?
Your giving supports the ministry of Live The Life Church, including weekly services and worship experiences, outreach and community impact, next generation ministries (kids & youth), and operational needs and church growth. Every dollar helps further the mission.
Will I receive a giving statement?
Yes. You'll receive a giving statement for your records, which can be accessed through your giving account or sent at year-end.
What if I need help with giving?
We're here to help! If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to reach out at giving@ltl.church.
