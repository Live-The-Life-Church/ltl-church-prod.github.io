The Wednesday night

Your week just got better.

Come as you are. Meet people who get it. Worship Jesus, hear a message built for your life, and find friends who will walk with you.

Find your people
WhenWed · 7 PM
WhoAges 12–18
WhereLTL Campus

Jesus.
Friends.
Discipleship.

Not a lecture. Not a pep rally. A night where students meet Jesus for real—and find people who have their back.

What to expect

A night worth showing up for.

Drive onto the property and use the designated student drop-off location. Here’s how the night flows:

01

Hangout

Doors open early for games, music, and time with friends before service starts.

02

Worship + Word

Students worship together, then receive age-appropriate teaching built for real life.

03

Pickup

Parents can line up at 8:00 PM. Students are dismissed at 8:30 PM.

You belong here

Show up as yourself.

Whether church is familiar or completely new, there is room for you at LTL Youth.
