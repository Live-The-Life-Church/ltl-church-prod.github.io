Not a lecture. Not a pep rally. A night where students meet Jesus for real — and find people who actually have their back.

What to expect

A night worth showing up for.

When you arrive, drive onto the property and drop your student off at the designated drop-off location. Here's how the night flows:

01

Hangout

Doors open early — games, music, and a supervised hangout before service kicks off.

02

Worship + Word

All students worship together, then split into middle school and high school for age-appropriate teaching.

03

Pickup

Parents can line up at 8:00 PM — students are dismissed at 8:30 PM, loud and happy.
