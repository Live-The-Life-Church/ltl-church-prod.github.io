Why We Go

Life change doesn't stop at our city limits.

Missions is not a side project — it's the heartbeat of the Church. Every trip, every gift, and every prayer extends the reach of the Gospel to nations we may never see, through leaders we get to call family.

01

Evangelism

We go so that all people can experience life change through Jesus Christ — carrying the Gospel to cities and villages far beyond our own.

02

Leadership Development

We invest in local pastors and leaders — equipping, encouraging, and strengthening the churches already planted in each nation.

03

Building Facilities

We put hands to the work — helping construct and restore places of worship where communities can gather, grow, and be discipled.
