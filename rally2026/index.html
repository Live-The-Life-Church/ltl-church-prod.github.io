What to expect

More than just a camp.

The Rally is where LTL Kids comes alive after hours. We provide more than childcare — we create a fun, engaging experience that clearly communicates biblical truth in a safe, age-specific environment.

Fun that fits kids

High-energy games, creative activities, and memorable moments built around how kids actually learn.

Worship & Bible teaching

Biblical truths presented in a fun, engaging way that sticks long after the final night.

Safe & age-specific

Every child is checked in securely and placed in an environment designed just for their age.

Bring a friend

The Rally is always better with friends. Invite a neighbor, classmate, or cousin to join.
